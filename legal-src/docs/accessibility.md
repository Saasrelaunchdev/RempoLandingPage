> **Something not working for you?** Email {{supportEmail}} with the page and the problem. We will reply within 2 business days and help you get what you need another way in the meantime.

## 1. Our commitment {#commitment}

We want everyone, including people with disabilities, to be able to use our website and services. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## 2. What we have done {#measures}

- Pages use meaningful headings, labels, and landmarks, so screen readers can navigate them.
- Every form field has a visible label, errors are announced, and forms work with a keyboard.
- Text and controls meet WCAG contrast levels in both the light and dark themes.
- Animations respect your device's reduced motion setting, and everything can be read without them.
- Illustrations that carry meaning have text descriptions; decorative ones are hidden from screen readers.
- We test our pages with automated accessibility checks and keyboard testing when they change.

## 3. Known limitations {#limitations}

- Some illustrations show sample data. Their meaning is also given in text nearby.
- Content from other companies, such as embedded videos and our payment processor's checkout page, is covered by those companies' own accessibility commitments.

## 4. Other formats {#formats}

If you need any of our legal documents or reports in another format, such as large print or plain text, email {{supportEmail}} and we will provide it.

## 5. Feedback {#feedback}

Tell us about any barrier you find at {{supportEmail}}. Please include the page address and what happened. We treat accessibility problems as bugs and fix them as quickly as we can.
