> **In plain words.** You see the full price before you pay. Plans renew automatically until you cancel. You can cancel at any time, online or by email, and you keep the service until the end of the period you paid for. This policy is part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. What you pay {#price}

Before you pay, checkout shows the full price: the recurring fee for each location or Google profile, how often it is charged (monthly or yearly), any one-time setup fee, any add-ons you chose, and any commitment that applies. Your receipt repeats these terms. Prices don't include taxes, which are added where the law requires.

## 2. When you are charged {#when}

You pay the setup fee and your first billing period at checkout. After that, your plan renews automatically and is charged on the same day each month (or year, for annual plans) until you cancel. By subscribing, you authorize these recurring charges to the payment method you gave us.

**Annual plans:** we email a reminder between 15 and 45 days before each renewal, with the renewal date, the amount, and how to cancel.

**Price changes:** we email you at least 30 days before a price increase takes effect. You can cancel before it does.

## 3. How to cancel {#cancel}

You can cancel at any time, without calling us:

- **online,** in your billing portal{{portalSuffix}}; or
- **by email,** to {{supportEmail}} with the word "Cancel" and your business name.

Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period. You keep the Services until then, and you won't be charged again. We confirm every cancellation by email.

When you cancel, we may offer you one alternative, such as a smaller plan or a pause. You can decline it and finish cancelling in the same step. We never make you talk to us, wait, or jump through extra steps to cancel.

## 4. Refunds {#refunds}

- **Monthly and annual fees** are not refunded for partial periods.
- **Setup fees** are not refunded once setup work has started, because they pay for that work. If we can't start your setup for reasons on our side, we refund the setup fee in full.
- **Guarantees:** if your plan includes a guarantee, its exact terms are shown at checkout and on your receipt. A guarantee applies when you have completed your onboarding steps on time (connecting your software, giving profile access, sending your photo, and approving your texting details). To claim it, email {{supportEmail}} within 30 days of the missed promise. The guarantee's remedy is the remedy for missing it.
- **If we end the Services for our convenience,** or a change materially reduces the Services you pay for, we refund the fees you prepaid for the unused period.
- **Where the law requires a refund,** we give it.

## 5. Commitments and waived fees {#commitments}

Some plans waive a fee (such as the setup fee) in exchange for a commitment (such as 12 months). You can still cancel at any time. If you cancel before the commitment ends, the waived fee becomes due, as checkout and your receipt state. Nothing else becomes due.

## 6. Failed payments {#failed}

If a payment fails, we email you and retry it. If it is still unpaid 10 days after our email, we may pause the Services until it is paid. We restart them as soon as the payment goes through.

## 7. Questions and disputes {#questions}

If a charge looks wrong, email {{supportEmail}} within 60 days, and we will look into it right away. Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank, so we can fix it faster.
