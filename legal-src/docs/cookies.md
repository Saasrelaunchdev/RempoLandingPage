> **In short.** Our pages use a few strictly necessary storage items that never leave your browser for advertising. Advertising and analytics tools, including Meta's pixel, load only if you choose "Allow," never if your browser sends Global Privacy Control, and you can change your mind at any time on [Your Privacy Choices](/legal/privacy-choices).

## 1. What these technologies are {#what}

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies include browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage), pixels (small pieces of code that send information to another company when a page loads or an action happens), and server-side events (information our server sends to a partner). This notice uses "cookies" for all of them.

## 2. Strictly necessary {#necessary}

These keep the pages working. They stay in your browser, are not used for advertising, and don't need your consent.

| Name | Type | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| lp-theme | localStorage | Remembers whether you chose the light or dark theme | Until you clear it |
| lp-visit, rm-visit | sessionStorage | A random visit ID that links the steps of a form to each other and to a checkout, so a purchase can be matched to the visit | Until you close the tab |
| rm-gc | sessionStorage | Keeps your Gap Check answers and progress if you reload the page | Until you close the tab |
| rm-attr | sessionStorage | Remembers the ad or link that brought you (campaign tags and Meta's click ID) so it can be sent with your form | Until you close the tab |
| rm-consent | localStorage | Remembers your cookie choice and when you made it | Until you clear it or change your choice |

## 3. Advertising: Meta pixel {#meta-pixel}

**When it runs:** on our ad landing pages, only after you choose "Allow," and never when your browser sends Global Privacy Control.

**What it does:** it tells Meta Platforms which of our pages you visit and which steps you take, such as starting the Gap Check, reaching each question, requesting a report, booking a call, or opening checkout, with details like your score tier, whether your answers fit who we serve, and your trade. Meta uses this to measure our ads and show them to businesses like yours.

**What Meta receives:** the page address, your browser and device details, your IP address, the event details above, and Meta's cookie IDs. Meta may set its own cookies (such as _fbp and _fbc) in your browser.

**Third parties and you:** third parties, including Meta, may use cookies, web beacons, and other storage technologies to collect or receive information from our pages and elsewhere on the internet, and use it to provide measurement services and target ads. Meta's use is governed by [Meta's privacy policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

## 4. Advertising: Meta Conversions API (from our server) {#capi}

**When it runs:** when you send a form whose answers fit who we serve, unless you opted out or your browser sends Global Privacy Control.

**What we send:** that a lead or a booked call happened and your score tier, with your email address, first name, city, and visit ID in hashed form, your country, your IP address, your browser details, and Meta's click and cookie IDs. We never send your phone number.

## 5. Analytics: Microsoft Clarity {#clarity}

**When it runs:** only if we turn it on, only after you choose "Allow," and never with Global Privacy Control. It is off today.

**What it does:** it records how visitors use a page (clicks, taps, scrolling, and mouse movement) so we can find what is confusing or broken. We configure it to mask what you type into forms. Recordings are kept for up to 30 days. Microsoft may set its own cookies (such as _clck and _clsk).

## 6. Other services {#others}

- **Video:** if a page shows a video from YouTube, it uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, which doesn't set cookies until you press play. After that, YouTube may set its own cookies.
- **Checkout:** when you pay, you go to our payment processor's checkout page, which uses its own cookies for security and fraud prevention under its own policy.

## 7. How to control cookies {#control}

- **Our choices:** use [Your Privacy Choices](/legal/privacy-choices), or the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of our ad landing pages, to allow or decline advertising and analytics tools at any time.
- **Global Privacy Control:** if your browser or an extension sends it, we treat it as declining advertising and analytics tools and as an opt-out of sale and sharing.
- **Your browser:** you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary storage may break parts of our pages.
- **Ad preferences:** you can control how Meta uses information for ads in [Meta's ad preferences](https://accountscenter.facebook.com/ad_preferences), and opt out of interest-based ads from participating companies at [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices) (Digital Advertising Alliance) and [optout.networkadvertising.org](https://optout.networkadvertising.org) (Network Advertising Initiative).
