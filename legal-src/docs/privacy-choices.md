## What opting out does {#effect}

- Our pages won't load Meta's pixel or session analytics on this browser.
- If you send us a form from this browser, our server won't send anything about you to Meta, and we record your opt-out with your request.
- It doesn't stop the emails or texts you agreed to. Use the unsubscribe link or reply STOP for those.

Your choice is stored in this browser only. If you use another browser or device, or clear your browser data, make your choice again there.

## Opt out for information we already have {#email}

To opt out of sale and sharing for information we already hold about you (for example, from a form you sent before), email {{privacyEmail}} with the subject "Do Not Sell or Share" and the email address or phone number you used. We don't need to verify your identity for an opt-out, and we will act on it within 15 business days.

## Global Privacy Control {#gpc}

Global Privacy Control (GPC) is a setting in some browsers and extensions that tells websites you want to opt out of the sale and sharing of your personal information. We honor it as a valid opt-out on all our pages. If your browser sends GPC, the "Allow" button above stays off; to allow advertising tools, turn off GPC first.
