> **At a glance.** We collect what you type into our forms (like your name, business, email, and phone), how you use our pages, and public information about businesses. We use it to give you the race, report, or call you asked for, to run our services, and to measure our ads. We don't sell personal information for money. If you allow it, Meta's pixel helps us measure our ads, which California law calls "sharing"; you can opt out at any time on our [Your Privacy Choices](/legal/privacy-choices) page, and we honor Global Privacy Control. We never share your mobile number or texting consent with anyone for their marketing.

## 3. How we use personal information {#use}

We use personal information to:

- **provide what you asked for:** build and show your review race and email it to you with its follow-ups and updates, prepare and email your Scorecard, and schedule and hold your call;
- **communicate with you:** reply to you, send the emails and texts you agreed to, and send service notices;

- **use AI tools** to help draft replies, posts, and summaries as part of the services, and to write the notes and reply drafts in a review race from public reviews; and
- **meet legal duties and enforce our agreements,** including keeping records of consent and opt-outs.

We use personal information only for the purposes described here, or for purposes compatible with the context in which we collected it. We don't use your information to make decisions about you that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

## 6. Advertising, "sale," and "sharing" {#sharing}

**We don't sell personal information for money.** California law also treats disclosing personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as "sharing." We do that in two ways, and only for measuring and improving our own ads:

- **Meta pixel (in your browser).** On our ad landing pages, Meta's pixel loads only after you choose "Allow" in our cookie notice. It tells Meta which of our pages you visit and which steps you take, so we can see which ads work and show our ads to businesses like yours.
- **Meta Conversions API (from our server).** When you unlock a review race or book a call and haven't opted out, our server may tell Meta that a lead or a booked call came from one of our ads. We send your email address, first name, city, and visit ID in hashed (scrambled) form, along with your country, your IP address, your browser details, and Meta's click ID. We send this only for businesses that fit who we serve, never your phone number, and never if you have opted out or your browser sends Global Privacy Control.

**Opt out at any time** on our [Your Privacy Choices](/legal/privacy-choices) page. We also treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a valid opt-out of sale and sharing: with it, our pages don't load Meta's pixel or session analytics, and our server sends nothing about you to Meta. Meta's own handling of information is described in [Meta's privacy policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/), and you can control the ads you see in [Meta's ad preferences](https://accountscenter.facebook.com/ad_preferences).

We don't knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

## 9. Your California privacy rights {#rights}

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

- **know and access** the personal information we have collected about you, including the categories, sources, purposes, and recipients, and to get a copy in a portable format;
- **delete** personal information we collected from you, subject to legal exceptions;
- **correct** inaccurate personal information;
- **opt out of the sale or sharing** of your personal information;
- **limit our use of sensitive personal information** (we already use it only for the purposes the law allows, so we offer no separate link); and
- **not be discriminated against** for using any of these rights.

**How to make a request.** Email {{privacyEmail}} with your request, or use our [Your Privacy Choices](/legal/privacy-choices) page for opt-outs. We will confirm receipt within 10 business days and respond within 45 days. If we need more time (up to another 45 days), we will tell you why. To protect you, we verify requests by matching information you give us (such as your email address or phone number) with what we have, and we may ask for more if needed. We don't require verification for opt-out requests.

**Authorized agents.** You may use an authorized agent to make a request. We may ask the agent for your signed permission and ask you to verify your identity with us directly.

**Other states.** If you live in another state with a consumer privacy law, you may have similar rights. Email us and we will honor your request as your state's law requires. We extend these choices to all our visitors where we reasonably can.

**Shine the Light.** California Civil Code section 1798.83 lets California residents ask about disclosures to third parties for their direct marketing. We don't disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct marketing.

## 10. Notice of financial incentive {#incentive}

Our Review Race and Scorecard are free. To see your full race or receive a Scorecard, you give us your contact details and, for the Scorecard, answers about your business. California law may treat a free offer like this as a financial incentive.

- **What you give and get:** you give us your first name and email (for the Scorecard, also your business name, city, and answers); we give you a comparison of your Google reviews and presence with the businesses near you. Booking a call adds your phone number.
- **How to opt in:** by sending the form. **How to withdraw:** unsubscribe from our emails, or ask us to delete your information, at any time. You can keep the race or Scorecard you received.
- **Value:** our good-faith estimate of the value of this information to Rempo is equal to our cost of producing the race or Scorecard you receive, which we calculate from the staff time and software costs (such as search and AI services) of preparing and delivering it.

## 12. Customers of our clients {#end-customers}

When a business that uses Rempo sends you a review request, we process your name, contact details, and the date of your service on that business's behalf. We use them only to send that business's messages and run its services, never for our own marketing, and we never sell them.

- **To stop messages:** reply STOP to a text, or use the unsubscribe link in an email. We honor it for that business across our system.
- **To access or delete your information:** contact the business you dealt with. If you contact us, we will pass your request to that business and help it respond.
- **Public reviews:** if you post a public review of a business we work for, we may read it and draft a reply on the business's behalf. Replies never reveal private details about you.

## 13. Business contacts we reach out to {#research}

We sometimes contact businesses that may benefit from Rempo. To do that, we collect business contact information (such as name, role, business email, and business phone) and public facts about the business (such as its Google rating, review count, and how recently it was reviewed) from public websites, Google Business Profiles, professional networks, and business data providers. We use it only to contact the business about our services. Every email we send includes a way to opt out, and we honor opt-outs across all our sending addresses within 10 business days, usually the same day. We don't cold text or cold call mobile numbers.

## 14. How we protect information {#security}

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards suited to the information, including encryption in transit, access limited to people who need it, multi-factor authentication on accounts that hold personal information, a lead database that is not publicly readable, and storing only a one-way hash of IP addresses. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as the law requires.
