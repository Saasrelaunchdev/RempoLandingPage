> **Why this matters.** Breaking review or texting rules can get a business's reviews deleted, its Google profile restricted, its texting blocked, and its owners fined. These rules protect you, your customers, and us. They are part of our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms).

## 1. Ask honestly {#honest-asking}

The Federal Trade Commission's Trade Regulation Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 C.F.R. Part 465) and Google's review policies set the rules. Rempo runs every program this way, and you agree to follow the same rules outside Rempo:

- **Ask everyone the same way.** Every eligible customer gets the same request. No surveys, star ratings, or sentiment checks that decide who gets the review link (often called "review gating").
- **No incentives.** Don't offer anything of value (discounts, refunds, gifts, entries, payments, or credits) for a review, to customers or to staff. Referral rewards, if you offer them, are for referrals only and never tied to a review.
- **No scripted reviews.** Don't tell customers what to write, which rating to give, or which employee to name.
- **No staff quotas.** No review targets, contests, or leaderboards for employees.
- **No fake or insider reviews.** Don't write, buy, sell, or swap reviews. Don't post reviews of your own business, or ask owners, staff, or their families to post them.
- **No on-site pressure.** Don't ask customers to review you on your device or at a kiosk on your premises.

## 2. Don't suppress reviews {#no-suppression}

- Don't threaten, harass, or intimidate anyone to remove a review or stop them from posting one, and don't make false claims to a platform to get an honest review removed.
- Don't use contract terms that ban, restrict, or penalize honest reviews. The Consumer Review Fairness Act and California Civil Code section 1670.8 prohibit them.
- When you show reviews (on your website or in our review widget), don't present a hand-picked set as all or most of your reviews. Label them for what they are, for example "Recent reviews from Google."

## 3. Use reviews fairly {#using-reviews}

- Quote reviewers by first name or initials only, and don't edit a review in a way that changes its meaning.
- **Healthcare:** get the patient's written authorization before using their review or likeness in marketing, where HIPAA or state law requires it. Replies never confirm that someone is a patient and never mention care.
- **Law firms:** follow your state's rules on testimonials and disclaimers, and never reveal client information in a reply.
- **Financial advisers:** include the disclosures the SEC Marketing Rule requires for testimonials.

## 4. Message lawfully {#messaging}

- Send texts only to people who agreed to receive texts from your business, as described in our [SMS and Messaging Terms](/legal/sms). Never give us purchased, rented, or scraped lists.
- Honor every opt-out, including opt-outs your customers give you in person, by phone, or by email, and tell us about them.
- Every message names your business and includes opt-out instructions. Don't hide who is sending.
- Don't use texting for content the carriers prohibit, such as content about sex, hate, alcohol, firearms, tobacco, cannabis, or illegal drugs, or for debt collection, loans, or other high-risk financial offers.
- Don't send messages with misleading content, links that disguise where they lead, or public link shorteners.
- Keep texts within daytime hours. We schedule them between 9 a.m. and 8 p.m. in your local time zone.

## 5. Use AI features responsibly {#ai}

- Don't use AI features to deceive. Where the law requires, tell people they are talking with an automated assistant, and never present an assistant as a person or as a licensed professional.
- Keep call recording notices in place wherever the AI receptionist records calls, and get any consent the law requires.
- Don't use AI features to make outbound calls with an artificial or prerecorded voice without the prior express consent the law requires.
- Review and approve the guidelines AI drafts follow. You are responsible for content you approve or publish.
