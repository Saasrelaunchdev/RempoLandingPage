## 1. Subprocessors for client customer data {#client-data}

These providers may process information about our clients' customers (names, contact details, completed work, and messages) to deliver the Services. Some are used only for certain plans or features.

| Provider | What it does for Rempo | Information it handles |
|---|---|---|
| HighLevel, Inc. | Our client platform: contact records, review requests and reminders by text and email, review monitoring and replies, AI drafting and receptionist features, and the providers HighLevel uses to deliver messages | End Customer names and contact details, message content, opt-outs, reviews, call records |
| Zapier, Inc. | Moves completed-job records from a client's software to our client platform, when a direct connection isn't available | End Customer names, contact details, and job details |
| NiftyImages | Renders the personalized request image with the client's team photo and the customer's first name | The client's photo and the End Customer's first name |
| Google LLC | Google Business Profile, where reviews are read and replies are posted through the client's own profile | Reviews, reviewer public names, replies |
| Your website host | Hosts the website we build for you, if your plan includes one (named in your Order) | Website content and form submissions |

**Healthcare practices:** we send patient information only through providers that have signed a business associate agreement with us, as our [Business Associate Agreement](/legal/baa) requires. A provider that hasn't signed one is turned off for that practice.

## 2. Service providers for our website, leads, and clients {#rempo-data}

These providers process information about visitors, prospects, and client contacts (not client customer data).

| Provider | What it does for Rempo | Information it handles |
|---|---|---|
| Vercel Inc. | Hosts our website and the code that receives our forms | Page requests, form submissions, IP addresses in short-lived logs |
| Supabase, Inc. | Our lead database | Form submissions, consent records, a hash of your IP address |
| Resend | Sends our emails, including reports, call confirmations, and lead alerts to our team | Names, email addresses, email content |
| Slack Technologies, LLC | Alerts our team to new leads | Form submissions |
| Stripe, Inc. | Processes payments and runs the billing portal | Billing name, email, payment details (we never see full card numbers) |
| Clay Labs, Inc. | Researches businesses that may benefit from Rempo | Business contact details and public business information |
| Microsoft Corporation (Clarity) | Session analytics on our pages, only if we turn it on and you allow it | How visitors use our pages, with typed text masked |

## 3. Advertising partners {#advertising}

| Partner | What it does | Information it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Meta Platforms, Inc. | Measures and improves our ads on Facebook and Instagram, through its pixel (only if you allow it) and its Conversions API (unless you opt out) | Described in our [Cookie and Tracking Notice](/legal/cookies). Never your phone number |

Meta is a third party, not our service provider. You can opt out on our [Your Privacy Choices](/legal/privacy-choices) page.

## 4. Changes {#changes}

We review this list when we add or change a provider. Each provider is bound by a written contract that limits its use of personal information, as our [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa) describes.
