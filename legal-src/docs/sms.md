## 1. Texts from Rempo to you {#rempo-texts}

**Program name:** {{smsProgram}}.

**What we send:** if you tick the text box when you book a call, we text you to confirm the call, remind you of it, or move it. If you are a client and opt in, we also text you service notices, such as setup steps that need you and alerts about new reviews.

**How you opt in:** by ticking the optional text box on our booking form, or by giving written consent during onboarding. The box is never pre-ticked and isn't required to book a call or buy.

**How often:** for a booked call, up to 3 texts per booking. For client service notices, the number varies with your account activity.

**Costs:** message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

**Opt out:** reply STOP (or cancel, end, quit, unsubscribe, revoke, or opt out) to any text. We will send one text confirming that you have opted out, and then no more texts from that program. You can also opt out by emailing {{supportEmail}}. To start again, reply START or tick the box again.

**Help:** reply HELP, or email {{supportEmail}}.{{phoneSentence}}

**Carriers:** supported on major US carriers. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

**Privacy:** we never sell, rent, or share your mobile number or your text messaging consent with anyone for their marketing. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy#sms).

## 2. Review-request texts sent for our clients {#client-texts}

Our clients are businesses, such as HVAC and plumbing companies, that use Rempo to ask their customers for reviews. If you got a text after a job, visit, or case, here is how it works.

- **Who it's from:** the business you dealt with. Rempo sends it on that business's behalf, and the message names the business.
- **What it says:** a thank-you and a link to leave an honest review. At most one reminder follows if you don't respond, and then we stop. Every customer gets the same request. We never offer rewards for reviews or tell you what to write.
- **When:** we schedule texts between 9 a.m. and 8 p.m. in the business's local time zone.
- **Opt out:** reply STOP. We stop texts from that business through Rempo right away and send one confirmation. Reply HELP for help.
- **Your information:** the business's own privacy policy governs it. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy#end-customers) for how we handle it as the business's service provider.

## 3. For clients: consent to text your customers {#client-consent}

You may only give us the numbers of customers who agreed to receive texts from your business. Collect that consent where you collect the number (a booking form, a work order, an intake form, or a signed agreement), and keep a record of it. Carrier registration (A2P 10DLC) requires an opt-in like this, and the phone carriers check it.

**Model consent wording for your forms.** Adapt the bracketed parts, and show it next to the phone field with an unticked box or a clear statement the customer accepts:

> By giving us your mobile number, you agree to receive text messages from [Business name] about your service, including appointment updates and a request to review your visit. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. [Link to your privacy policy] [Link to your SMS terms]

**For healthcare practices:** don't put health details in the consent wording or in messages, and sign our [Business Associate Agreement](/legal/baa) before sending us patient contact details.

**For law firms:** get each client's consent to be contacted for a review before their details reach Rempo, for example with a consent line in your engagement or closing letter.

**Your privacy policy** must not say that you share texting opt-in data with third parties for their marketing. Our [Acceptable Use and Review Integrity Policy](/legal/acceptable-use) has the full rules.
