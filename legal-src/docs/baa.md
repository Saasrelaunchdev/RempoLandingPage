> **Who this is for.** Healthcare practices that are HIPAA covered entities. Patient contact details are protected health information, because they show that a person is a patient of the practice. This agreement must be in place before you send Rempo any of it. You accept it electronically at checkout or onboarding, or you can ask {{legalEmail}} for a copy to sign.

This Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") is between the healthcare practice that accepts it ("Covered Entity") and {{company}} ("Business Associate"). It supplements the [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) and takes effect when Covered Entity accepts it, electronically or by signature (the "Effective Date").

## 1. Definitions {#definitions}

Capitalized terms used but not defined here have the meanings given in the HIPAA Rules, including Breach, Data Aggregation, Designated Record Set, Disclosure, Electronic Protected Health Information, Health Care Operations, Individual, Minimum Necessary, Notice of Privacy Practices, Required by Law, Secretary, Security Incident, Subcontractor, Unsecured Protected Health Information, and Use.

- **"HIPAA Rules"** means the Privacy, Security, Breach Notification, and Enforcement Rules at 45 C.F.R. Parts 160 and 164, as amended, including by the HITECH Act.
- **"PHI"** means Protected Health Information, as defined at 45 C.F.R. 160.103, that Business Associate creates, receives, maintains, or transmits on behalf of Covered Entity.
- **"Services"** means the services Business Associate provides to Covered Entity under the Terms of Service.

## 2. Permitted uses and disclosures {#permitted}

Business Associate may Use and Disclose PHI only:

1. to perform the Services for Covered Entity, which include sending review requests and one reminder to patients after their visits, creating personalized request images, monitoring reviews and drafting replies that never confirm that a reviewer is a patient and never mention care, honoring opt-outs, and reporting to Covered Entity;
2. as Required by Law;
3. for the proper management and administration of Business Associate or to carry out its legal responsibilities, as permitted by 45 C.F.R. 164.504(e)(4). A Disclosure for these purposes is allowed only if it is Required by Law, or if Business Associate obtains reasonable written assurances from the recipient that the PHI will be held confidentially, Used or further Disclosed only as Required by Law or for the purpose for which it was Disclosed, and that the recipient will notify Business Associate of any instance in which the confidentiality of the PHI has been breached; and
4. to provide Data Aggregation services relating to Covered Entity's Health Care Operations, only if Covered Entity requests them in writing.

Business Associate will:

- Use, Disclose, and request only the Minimum Necessary PHI to accomplish the intended purpose;
- not Use or Disclose PHI in any manner that would violate Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 if done by Covered Entity, except as permitted by item 3 or 4 above;
- not sell PHI, or Use or Disclose it for marketing or fundraising;
- not de-identify PHI except as needed to perform the Services or as Covered Entity permits in writing; and
- not accept records protected by 42 C.F.R. Part 2.

## 3. Safeguards {#safeguards}

Business Associate will use appropriate safeguards, and comply with Subpart C of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 for Electronic Protected Health Information, to prevent Use or Disclosure of PHI other than as this BAA provides. This includes administrative, physical, and technical safeguards that reasonably and appropriately protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Electronic Protected Health Information.

## 4. Reporting {#reporting}

Business Associate will report to Covered Entity:

- **Use or Disclosure not allowed by this BAA,** of which it becomes aware, without unreasonable delay and no later than 10 business days after discovery;
- **Security Incidents** of which it becomes aware, on the same timeline. The parties agree that this section is notice, with no further notice required, of the ongoing existence of unsuccessful Security Incidents, such as pings and other broadcast attacks on firewalls, port scans, unsuccessful log-on attempts, denials of service, and any combination of these, as long as they don't result in unauthorized access, Use, or Disclosure of PHI; and
- **Breaches of Unsecured PHI,** as required by 45 C.F.R. 164.410, without unreasonable delay and no later than 10 business days after discovery (and never later than 60 calendar days after discovery). The report will include, to the extent known, the identity of each Individual affected and the other information Covered Entity needs to notify Individuals under 45 C.F.R. 164.404, supplemented as more information becomes available.

Business Associate will reasonably cooperate with Covered Entity in investigating and mitigating any harmful effect of a Use or Disclosure that violates this BAA, to the extent practicable.

## 5. Subcontractors {#subcontractors}

In accordance with 45 C.F.R. 164.502(e)(1)(ii) and 164.308(b)(2), Business Associate will ensure that any Subcontractor that creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on its behalf agrees in writing to the same restrictions, conditions, and requirements that apply to Business Associate under this BAA. Business Associate will route PHI only through Subcontractors that have signed such an agreement.

## 6. Individuals' rights {#individual-rights}

To the extent Business Associate maintains PHI in a Designated Record Set, it will, within 10 business days after Covered Entity's request:

- make the PHI available to Covered Entity so Covered Entity can meet its obligations under 45 C.F.R. 164.524 (access);
- make amendments to the PHI that Covered Entity directs under 45 C.F.R. 164.526; and
- provide the information Covered Entity needs to give an accounting of Disclosures under 45 C.F.R. 164.528.

If an Individual asks Business Associate directly for access, amendment, or an accounting, Business Associate will forward the request to Covered Entity within 10 business days.

## 7. Other obligations {#other}

- **Covered Entity's obligations.** To the extent Business Associate carries out one of Covered Entity's obligations under Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164, it will comply with the requirements of Subpart E that apply to Covered Entity in performing that obligation.
- **Books and records.** Business Associate will make its internal practices, books, and records relating to PHI available to the Secretary to determine compliance with the HIPAA Rules.
- **California law.** To the extent it applies, Business Associate will comply with the California Confidentiality of Medical Information Act (California Civil Code section 56 and following) in its handling of medical information received from Covered Entity.

## 8. Covered Entity's obligations {#ce-obligations}

Covered Entity will:

- notify Business Associate of any limitation in its Notice of Privacy Practices, any change in or revocation of an Individual's permission, and any restriction on Use or Disclosure it has agreed to, to the extent it affects Business Associate's Use or Disclosure of PHI;
- not ask Business Associate to Use or Disclose PHI in any manner that would not be permissible under Subpart E of 45 C.F.R. Part 164 if done by Covered Entity;
- send only the Minimum Necessary PHI (generally a patient's name, contact details, and visit date) and no clinical details; and
- obtain any patient authorization the HIPAA Rules or state law require before Business Associate uses a patient's review or likeness in marketing.

## 9. Term and termination {#term}

This BAA starts on the Effective Date and ends when all PHI is returned or destroyed under Section 10. If either party determines that the other has violated a material term of this BAA, it may give written notice. If the violation isn't cured within 30 days after the notice, the non-breaching party may terminate this BAA and the affected Services. If cure isn't possible, it may terminate immediately.

## 10. Return or destruction {#return}

When this BAA ends, Business Associate will return to Covered Entity or destroy all PHI it still maintains in any form, and keep no copies, within 90 days. If returning or destroying some PHI is infeasible (for example, a record of a patient's opt-out that must be kept so that patient is never contacted again, or data in backups awaiting their normal deletion), Business Associate will extend the protections of this BAA to that PHI and limit further Uses and Disclosures to the purposes that make return or destruction infeasible, for as long as it maintains the PHI.

## 11. General {#general}

- **Interpretation.** This BAA will be interpreted to permit compliance with the HIPAA Rules. A reference to a section of the HIPAA Rules means the section as in effect or as amended.
- **Amendment.** The parties will amend this BAA as needed to comply with changes to the HIPAA Rules. Business Associate may update this BAA to reflect changes in law by posting a new version and notifying Covered Entity.
- **Conflicts.** For PHI, this BAA controls over the Terms of Service and the [Data Processing Addendum](/legal/dpa).
- **No third-party beneficiaries.** Nothing in this BAA gives any rights to anyone other than the parties.
- **Survival.** Section 10 and Business Associate's obligations for PHI it keeps survive termination.
- **Liability.** Liability under this BAA is subject to the limits in the Terms of Service, to the extent permitted by law.
