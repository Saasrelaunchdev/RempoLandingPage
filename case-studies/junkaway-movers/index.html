01Before Rempo

The asking was there. The follow-up wasn't.

Over two years, Junkaway & Movers collected 32 Google reviews. The team was already asking for a review after each job.

Happy customers often said they'd leave one. Then they got busy, and forgot.

02What Rempo did

Rempo took over the follow-up.

Customers got a review request, and anyone who hadn't left one got a reminder. Nobody on the team had to keep track.

  1. 1

    A review request

    After the job, customers got a request to share their experience on Google.

  2. 2

    A reminder, if they hadn't

    Anyone who hadn't left a review yet got a reminder, and another chance to share their experience.

  3. 3

    Nothing for the team to track

    Nobody had to keep track of who still needed a reminder. The team kept working.

03The result

70 new five-star reviews in six months.

In their first six months with Rempo, Junkaway & Movers received 70 new five-star Google reviews, taking their total from 32 to 102.

That is more than twice as many reviews in six months as in the two years before.

Customers meant to leave a review. The reminder did the remembering.
